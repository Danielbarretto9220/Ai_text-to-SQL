## Business Glossary Terms

- **Disbursement Date**: The date a loan was sanctioned and its first funds released to the customer.
- **Interest Rate**: The annual percentage rate charged on the outstanding loan principal.
- **Loan Amount**: The principal sum sanctioned and disbursed to the customer at loan origination.
- **Loan Status**: The current lifecycle state of a loan (e.g. active, closed, defaulted).
- **NPA**: Non-Performing Asset — a loan on which the customer has fallen sufficiently behind on EMI payments that it is treated as in default.
- **Tenure**: The agreed repayment period of a loan, expressed in months.
